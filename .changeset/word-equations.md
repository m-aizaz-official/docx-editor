Add Word-style equation editing and rendering. Insert or edit equations via Insert > Equation using a linear (LaTeX-style) editor with a live preview, a structure palette, and a gallery of common equations. Equations now render as laid-out math (fractions, superscript/subscript, radicals, n-ary operators, delimiters, matrices) instead of a plain-text fallback, and round-trip as OMML. Built in-house with no new dependencies.
